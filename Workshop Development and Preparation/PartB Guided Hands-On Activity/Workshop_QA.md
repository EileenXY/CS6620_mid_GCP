# Google Cloud Vertex AI Workshop — Q&A Preparation

**Workshop Topic:** Building an AI-Powered Food Delivery Support Assistant with Google Cloud Vertex AI

### Q1. Why did we choose Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite?

We chose Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite because it is designed for fast, cost-efficient AI tasks.

Our customer support assistant mainly needs to interpret refund policies, evaluate delivery delays, and ask for missing information. These tasks do not require a large, computationally expensive model.

Flash-Lite is therefore a suitable choice for a short, hands-on workshop where students will run multiple prompts using their Google Cloud Free Trial accounts.

**Short Answer (for live presentation):**

We chose Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite because it is designed for low-latency, cost-efficient tasks and is suitable for our simple customer support application.
